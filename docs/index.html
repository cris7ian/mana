Where’d my mana go?

Check your spellbooks.

Codex, Hermes, Pi, OpenClaw and other tools can share a subscription. Their spells draw from the same limit. Mana shows your Codex and OpenCode Go usage together, so you know what’s left.

Want more spellbooks? We welcome provider contributions. Shoot us a PR

For terminal wizards

Cast from the command line.

The app includes the mana command. Link it to your PATH and check your usage without leaving the shell. 

mana --provider all --json

Hi, I'm Cristian, the creator of Mana. I built this app because I use my subscriptions across multiple tools and wanted a way to keep track of my usage easily.

I know there's other stuff in the market that does this better and even inside the tools I use, but I just wanted something simple and easy to use. If you're looking for a tool with all batteries included, this might not be it. For that I recommend Codexbar.

That being said, I think that my app is more than enough. The important thing about your mana is not tracking it, but using it wisely.
