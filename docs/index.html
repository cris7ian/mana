Why Mana

One place to track your subscriptions.

The same subscription can power Codex, Hermes, Pi, and other tools. Mana brings your Codex and OpenCode Go usage and reset times into one menu-bar view, no matter which tool you use.

Prefer the terminal?

There’s a CLI, too.

The installed app includes the mana command. Link it to your PATH to check usage from your shell. 

mana --provider all --json
