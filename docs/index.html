Save a spell for the boss fight.

Hi, I'm Cristian, the creator of Mana. I built this app because my subscriptions are spread across a handful of tools, and I wanted one place to keep an eye on all of them.

I know there's other stuff in the market that does this better, and the tools themselves come with something built in. I just wanted something simple that sits one click away. If you're looking for a tool with all batteries included, this might not be it. For that I recommend Codexbar.

That being said, I think that my app is more than enough. The important thing about your mana is not how much you have left, but how you spend it.

May you wander in wisdom.
