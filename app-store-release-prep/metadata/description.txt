Save your mana.

Your AI tools have limits. Mana puts them in one clear view, so you can see what’s left before your next task.

TWO SPELLBOOKS. ONE PLACE.

Check Codex and OpenCode Go usage on your iPhone. See your five-hour and weekly quotas, plus the monthly quota for OpenCode Go. The bars show what’s left, not what you’ve spent.

KNOW WHEN YOU’RE BACK.

See quota reset countdowns. Tap a countdown to see its date and time. Pull down to refresh when you want another look. Mana also checks every minute while the app is open.

KEEP IT CLOSE.

Add a Home Screen widget for both providers or just one. Add a Lock Screen widget for a selected quota. iOS decides when background and widget updates run; they are not live meters.

YOUR KEYS. YOUR IPHONE.

Connect Codex through browser approval with your OpenAI account. Connect OpenCode Go with your own API key. Credentials stay in your iPhone’s Keychain. Mana saves only the latest usage, not a history. Disconnect a provider to remove its saved credentials and usage.

Small, focused, and open source. No charts to study. No extra dashboard to manage. Just a place to check your mana.

Save a spell for the boss fight.

COMPATIBILITY

iPhone with iOS 18 or later. Live usage requires a supported provider account and an internet connection. Provider subscriptions and service access are separate from Mana.

The iPhone app supports Codex and OpenCode Go. It does not include Antigravity, notifications, multiple accounts per provider, usage history, or cloud sync.

Mana is an independent utility. It is not affiliated with or endorsed by OpenAI or OpenCode.
